# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

SQLUMEN is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, physical appearance,
race, religion, sexual orientation, or other protected characteristic.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Protecting private or sensitive information shared in good faith.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate intimidation or unwanted contact.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers should act fairly, document enforcement decisions, protect the
privacy of reporters, and avoid conflicts of interest.

## Reporting Process

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the private
contact method listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Do not use public issues
for sensitive reports. Include:

- A description of what happened.
- Relevant links, dates, and context.
- Names or usernames of those involved, if known.
- Any supporting evidence.
- The outcome you are seeking, if applicable.

Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible. Do not include
secrets, credentials, or unrelated private data.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. A private or public correction.
2. A warning.
3. A temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban from project spaces.
5. Escalation to platform or legal authorities when required.

The response will consider the circumstances, impact, prior behavior, and
whether the conduct was repeated. Maintainers may skip steps when necessary
to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, community channels, events, and private communication related to
the project. It also applies when someone is representing SQLUMEN or its
community in public.
