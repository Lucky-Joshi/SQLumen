# Explanation Templates

Structured templates keep Intelligence responses consistent and easy to render.

## Standard Template

```text
Summary
    ↓
Execution Order
    ↓
Important Joins
    ↓
Aggregations
    ↓
Warnings
    ↓
Optimization Suggestions
    ↓
Interview Tips
```

Sections should be omitted when no relevant evidence exists, rather than filled with speculation.

## Template Rules

- Deterministic warnings appear before model suggestions.
- Each claim links to a graph node or source location when possible.
- Unknown information is labeled explicitly.
- Beginner, intermediate, and expert modes may vary detail but retain the same factual boundaries.
